1. Product Context & Domain Immersion

1.1 The Indonesian Tech Hiring Reality

High-growth tech companies and digital banks across Indonesia face extreme application volumes (500–1,500 candidates per open software engineering vacancy). Talent acquisition teams spend over 70% of initial screening bandwidth reading templated resumes and running repetitive screening calls. Traditional automated screening (LeetCode-style algorithmic puzzles or multiple-choice trivia) penalizes pragmatic engineers who understand production trade-offs. The AI Interview Platform delivers true leverage by conducting dynamic, conversational technical interviews that test depth of reasoning while enforcing structured rubrics (L1–L5).

1.2 Legal & Ethical Mandate: UU PDP No. 27/2022

Candidates do not choose to be interviewed by AI; they are subjected to algorithmic evaluation. Under Indonesia's Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP):

  • Pasal 20 & 21: Explicit, informed consent is mandatory prior to recording biometric voice data and executing automated decision-making.
  • Pasal 35 & 39: Personal Identifiable Information (PII) including candidate names, voice audio, and verbatim transcripts must be shielded from leaking into server logs and APM telemetry.
  • Pasal 8: Candidates possess the right to transparency on evaluation criteria, necessitating verbatim quote evidence backing every score.

3. Architecture & Trade-off Analysis: Option A vs Option B

We evaluated two foundational architectures for the interview execution engine:

Evaluation Dimension Option A: Direct LLM Audio Stream (WebRTC/Bidi) Option B: Decoupled Async Event Pipeline (Selected)
Turn Latency Sub-second (~500ms) conversational rhythm. Moderate (~800–1200ms) per conversational turn.
Indonesian 4G Cellular Resilience Extremely Fragile: Packet drops terminate WebSocket connection; entire session is lost. Highly Resilient: Chunked audio streaming; automatic reconnect preserves full transcript state.
Operating Cost (OpEx) High (~$0.06/minute) for continuous raw multimodal audio tokens. Optimal (~$0.005/minute) for text turn inference with prompt caching.
UU PDP Legal Audit Trail Difficult to capture discrete, verifiable transcripts for statutory compliance. Native persistence: Every turn stored in PostgreSQL with exact millisecond timestamps.
Assessor Override Preservation Impaired; regeneration risks desynchronization with audio stream. Native relational integrity with AssessorOverride entity preservation.

5. AI Verification Moment (Senior Staff Critical Oversight)

The Flawed AI Proposal:

When asked to resolve P0-1, an AI assistant proposed saving overrides in an in-memory array, calling portfolio.portfolio_skills.destroy_all, and later updating the array records with override.update!(portfolio_skill: new_skill).

The Senior Staff Critique & Diagnosis:

  1. ActiveRecord Cascading Deletion: In PortfolioSkill, the relation is declared as has_one :assessor_override, dependent: :destroy. Calling destroy_all executed immediate SQL DELETE queries on all existing overrides. The in-memory array held frozen, destroyed records; updating them caused silent failures.
  2. Absence of Transaction Boundary: The AI proposal lacked ActiveRecord::Base.transaction. Any network or parsing failure halfway through left candidate skills permanently wiped.
  3. The Staff Engineer Fix: Extracted override attributes into detached ruby Hash value objects, executed destruction within an atomic transaction, and recreated records with preserved timestamps and notes.
